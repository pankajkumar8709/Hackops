HackOps 2026 Official Rules
Submission Deadline: The final submission deadline is 5:00 PM on the last day.
All teams must submit via the participant portal before the deadline.
Teams must include a GitHub repository URL, a working demo URL, and a description.
Late submissions receive a 10% penalty per hour.
The maximum team size is 4 members. AI-generated code must be disclosed in the README.
Prizes: First place wins $5000, second place $2500, third place $1000.
Judging criteria: 40% technical difficulty, 30% innovation, 30% presentation.
